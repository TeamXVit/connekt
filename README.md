# Connekt (Currently in Alpha)

**Connekt** is a campus-exclusive social platform designed for VIT-AP students. Whether you’re looking to ask or answer queries, find teammates for projects, coordinate travel with peers, or just share anonymously — Connekt connects ideas and people within the campus like never before.

🚀 **Live here**: [https://connekt4vitap.onrender.com](https://connekt4vitap.onrender.com)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Queries** – Ask or answer questions relevant to your college life.
- **Find a Teammate** – Collaborate on projects, clubs, or events.
- **Lost & Found** – Report or claim lost items within campus.
- **Travel Partner** – Find travel partners of the same gender for safety and convenience.
- **Anonymous Confessions** – Share experiences and thoughts anonymously.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React.js, Material UI (MUI)
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js
- **Database**: MongoDB
- **Image Storage**: Cloudinary
